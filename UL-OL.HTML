Steps to create a website

  1. Plan — Define purpose, audience, and pages.
  2. Set up — Pick a platform, register a domain, get hosting.
  3. Build — Choose a template, add content and design.
  4. Test & optimize — Check on devices, add SEO.
  5. Launch — Publish your site.

activities one should do in a day

  • Move — 20–30 min exercise + sunlight.
  • Fuel — Balanced meals, stay hydrated.
  • Focus — Tackle your top priority in deep work.
  • Connect — Social interaction, even brief
  • Rest — Wind down, 7–9 hrs sleep
